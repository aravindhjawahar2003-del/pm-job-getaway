## 🚀 PM Job Getaway — Free, verified PM jobs for freshers

I kept applying to PM roles that were already closed. So I built a board that only lists verified Product Management roles.

**What it is**
- Internships, fresher roles & 0–8 years PM jobs — India + International
- Every posting checked on its original page, re-verified every 12 hours
- Real closing dates · Save & track jobs · 7-step Quick Tour
- Free forever, no sign-up

**Live right now:** 87 open roles (73 India · 14 International) · 124 tracked · 259 hiring platforms
